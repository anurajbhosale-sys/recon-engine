# Teach-back

1. Look at the most recent changes (git diff against the last commit or the slice's files).
2. Ask 5 questions, ONE AT A TIME, waiting for each answer:
   - 1 "what does this do" question
   - 2 "why this and not X" questions
   - 1 "what breaks if..." failure question
   - 1 interviewer-style follow-up that goes one level deeper
3. After each answer: say what was right, what was missing, and the precise correct answer.
   Be strict — vague answers are not correct answers.
4. At the end, list the concepts that were weak and suggest what to read or rewatch
   (prefer official docs and video/talk resources).
Do not write his learning-log entry for him.
